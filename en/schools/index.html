For schools, classes and parent associations

A classroom library made from the books at home

Families share the books they already own with the class. Every copy goes back to its owner, children keep a reading diary and the teacher sees how the group is reading.

Free for families. iOS and Android, in Catalan, Spanish, English, French, Basque and Galician.

Everything a classroom library needs

A library without buying a single book

Each family chooses which books to share with the class. The catalogue shows what there is, what is available and who has each book.

  • Age filters: early years, lower, middle and upper primary
  • Available now, short reads and books in Catalan
  • Search by title or author

Every book finds its way home

Each copy has an ex-libris label with a QR code and a short code. Scanning it requests a loan or records the return. If a book turns up in the classroom, the code says whose it is.

Loans with dates, without paperwork

Requests, hand-overs and returns are all recorded, with the due date in plain sight.

  • Up to two 14-day renewals
  • Waiting list, with 48 hours’ priority when the book comes back
  • Automatic reminder 3 days before it is due

A reading diary instead of the paper log

The responsible adult logs each child’s reading: minutes with the timer or by hand, pages, and a rating with faces and tags such as “Funny” or “A bit scary”.

  • A personal goal of minutes per week and books per term, with no rankings

Reading certificate for the term or year

A summary of finished books, minutes and reading days, to save, print or send to the teacher. No rankings, no comparisons.

Teacher dashboard

The group’s figures: books contributed, loans, reading time and logged reads. The report exports to CSV for meetings, with no personal data about children.

Whole-class reads, chapter by chapter

The class reads the same book in sections. Each section’s discussion only opens for those who have reached it, and the teacher can add challenges and questions.

Polls and meetups

The class votes on the next book and the teacher calls the book talk. Families confirm whether they are going, get notified when it is scheduled and receive a reminder the day before.

Designed for children

No accounts for children

Children are profiles of the responsible adult, with just a first name and year of birth. No address, phone, location or photo.

Only your class

Everyone sees the books shared with the clubs they belong to. People join with the code the teacher gives them.

No ads

No advertising and we do not sell data. Data is stored in the European Union, and any content can be reported.

How to get started

  1. The teacher creates the club

    Choose “Class or school” as the club type and share the invitation code with families.

  2. Families add books

    They scan the ISBN with the camera, in bursts if they have many, and choose which ones to share with the class.

  3. Children read

    They borrow books, keep their reading diary and the class talks about them at book talks.

Questions from teachers and families

Do children need a phone or their own account?

No. Children are profiles of the responsible adult, who is the one using the app. Several adults can manage the same library with an invitation code.

How much do families pay?

Nothing. Whoever creates the club pays for the Club plan and every member gets full access.

What if a book gets lost?

Each copy has its own ex-libris label with a QR and short code. Whoever finds it can scan it to see whose it is and return it.

Can a family keep some books private?

Yes. Each family decides which books to share, and can mark some as private or organise them on shelves.

Can the teacher see what each child reads?

No. The dashboard only shows group figures. Each child’s reading is only visible to the adults of their library.

Which languages does it support?

Catalan, Spanish and English, plus French, Portuguese, Italian, Basque and Galician, which download when you pick the language.
