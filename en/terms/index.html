LectoClub terms of use

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Service and provider

LectoClub is a service of ArnoHealth S.L. (onmedic), Barcelona, Spain. Contact: lectoclub@onmedic.org. It helps you organise physical books, share them with clubs, keep track of loans and reading, and discuss books. It doesn’t distribute the text of books, sell copies or deliver anything.

2. Account and children’s profiles

Accounts must be managed by someone aged 18 or over, who is responsible for how the account is used, for who joins their libraries and for being authorised to manage any children’s profiles. Children don’t have their own accounts. Keep access to your account safe and share invitation codes only with people who should join the club.

3. Books and catalogue

A work, an edition (identified by ISBN) and a physical copy are different things. Adding an ISBN gives you no rights over the work or over other people’s copies. Only add books you are entitled to manage. Records and covers from external sources may contain errors or be subject to third-party rights. Cover photos you upload must be yours or ones you are allowed to share.

4. Clubs, loans and bookshops

Each member chooses which copies to share with each club. Hand-over, loan period, condition and return are agreed between the adults involved; LectoClub doesn’t guarantee a copy’s availability and isn’t responsible for loss or damage. In a bookshop club, the organisers may turn off lending and link to their shop; purchases are made directly with the bookshop.

5. Plans and subscriptions

LectoClub has a free plan with limits (on clubs, members and books) and paid plans (Personal and Club), bought in the app through the App Store or Google Play under their payment terms.

  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price until you cancel them from your store account, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • If there is a free trial, you’re charged when it ends unless you cancel first.
  • With the Club plan, every active member of the club has full access while the subscription is active.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
  • Free-plan limits don’t apply to accounts created before 21 November 2026.

6. Content and acceptable use

A club’s comments, ratings and activities are visible to its members. Only post what you have the right to share, don’t include sensitive data or anything that identifies a child, and use the spoiler warning when it applies. Harassment, illegal content, impersonation, spam and infringing other people’s rights are not allowed. You can report content and block people in the app, or write to us.

7. Availability

You need an internet connection to sign in and sync. There may be maintenance, interruptions and changes to features. If a change affects something you have paid for, we’ll tell you in advance.

8. Leaving

You can stop using the service and ask for your account to be deleted (how). Leaving doesn’t cancel your store subscription or your obligation to return any books you have on loan.

9. Liability and governing law

These terms don’t limit the rights consumers have by law. They are governed by Spanish and European law. If something goes wrong, please write to us first at lectoclub@onmedic.org. We’ll publish changes here and announce important ones.
