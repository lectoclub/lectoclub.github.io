Everything a book club does

Book of the month

The organiser picks the book, the dates and the meetup, and everyone gets notified. Each member rates it and joins the general discussion.

Spoiler-free discussion topics

One thread for the characters, one for the setting, one for the ending… Topics about the ending stay hidden until each reader chooses to open them.

Chapter-by-chapter reads

Split the book into sections. Each section’s discussion only opens once a reader marks they have reached it, so nobody gives the plot away.

Vote on the next book

Polls with the options you suggest and the vote count in plain sight.

In-person or online meetups

Call the meetup with a place, a video call link or both. Everyone confirms whether they are coming and you can see who will be there.

  • The whole club is notified when it is scheduled
  • Automatic reminder the day before
  • If it is cancelled, those who were coming find out

The books at home, shared

Add books by pointing your camera at the barcode or QR code on the back cover — which every book has today — and arrange them on shelves. Share your whole library with the club, just some shelves, or only with certain people.

  • Loans with due dates, renewals and a waiting list
  • If the group doesn’t want loans, they can be turned off

The club’s history

Past books, ratings and discussions stay open so you can keep talking about them.

Your club, your way

No ads

No advertising and we do not sell data. You can block people and report any content.

How to get started

  1. Create the club

    Choose “Readers’ group”, give it a name and share the code with the group.

  2. Pick the first book

    Directly or with a poll. Add the dates and call the first meetup.

  3. Read and talk

    Discuss each section spoiler-free, lend each other books and see you at the meetup.

Questions from reading groups

Does everyone have to pay?

No. The organiser pays and everyone else gets full access. If you are 6 or fewer and the free plan is enough, nobody pays.

Can we use LectoClub without lending books?

Yes. The club admin can turn loans off in the club options, and you keep the book of the month, discussions and meetups.

How do we stop someone spoiling the ending?

Topics marked as spoilers stay hidden until each reader opens them, and in chapter-by-chapter reads each section’s discussion only opens for those who have reached it.

Can we meet by video call?

Yes. Each meetup can have a place, a Meet, Zoom or Teams link, or both.

Can I be in more than one club?

Yes, and choose which books you share with each one.
