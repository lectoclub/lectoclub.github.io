Your bookshop’s book club, in every reader’s pocket

Pick the book of the month, open the discussion topics and call the meetup. Readers buy the book from your shop in one tap, and beyond the in-person session, all the knowledge and discussions from every read stay centralised and preserved for the bookshop. The bookshop pays; readers join for free.

What your readers see

Discussion topics you open

Characters, setting, the ending… Each topic has its own thread with replies, and the conversation continues between meetups.

No spoilers for those still reading

Topics about the ending stay hidden until each reader chooses to open them.

All the club’s knowledge, kept by the bookshop

In-person meetups end, but the ideas, ratings and discussion threads stay centralised in the club. The bookshop builds a living archive of every read that can be revisited, used to recommend books and shared with new readers.

A club made for a bookshop

When you create your account, choose “Bookshop or organisation”. The clubs you create are already set up for a bookshop.

  • Buy in your shop

    Add your website and the catalogue and every book page show “Buy it at the bookshop”.

  • No lending between readers

    Bookshop clubs start with loans turned off. If you ever want them, switch them on in the club options.

  • Book of the month

    Pick the book, the reading dates and the meetup. Every member gets notified.

  • Meetups with RSVPs

    In the shop or by video call. Each reader says whether they are coming and gets a reminder the day before.

  • Organising team

    Make your booksellers organisers: they manage the club’s content without touching members or the plan.

  • As many clubs as you run

    Children’s, crime fiction, in English… Each club has its own invitation code and readers.

        Questions from bookshops

        Do readers have to pay?

        No. The bookshop pays the per-seat plan and the readers in its clubs get full access, for free.

        What does the free plan include?

        One club of up to 6 people with the book of the month, general discussion and ratings. Discussion topics, meetups, chapter-by-chapter reads, polls and history are part of the Club plan.

        Where do readers buy the books?

        On your website. The button goes to the address you set in the club options, and the sale stays yours.

        Do we need to talk to anyone to get started?

        No. Everything happens in the app: choose “Bookshop or organisation”, create the club and share the invite link. The one-minute video below shows it step by step.

        Do we need a card for the trial?

        No. The 30 days start when you create your first club, with no card and no automatic charge. Without a plan, clubs go back to the free version: no more than 6 readers can join, and you can’t schedule meetings, open discussion topics or run polls, and the history is hidden. Everything is kept and comes back as soon as you subscribe.

        Does sending the form commit me to anything?

        No. It is used to answer your question. It does not sign you up to any service or newsletter.
