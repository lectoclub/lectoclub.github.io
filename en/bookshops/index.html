Questions from bookshops

Can we activate a branded space now?

Not yet. This is a proposed pilot in preparation. The current app already has club tools; the branded pilot and shared coverage across multiple clubs must be agreed and built before activation.

How are members counted?

Each linked person counts once across all the bookshop’s clubs. Administrators do not use a place.

What happens if we exceed a tier?

We will propose an upgrade for you to accept. There are no automatic overage charges or tier changes.

Do readers have to pay?

The proposal is for the bookshop to pay so that members of its space can join without individual subscriptions.

Where are books purchased?

Purchase links will lead to your website. Your bookshop continues to handle sales and the relationship with the buyer.

Does the contact form commit us to anything?

No. It lets us answer your question and discuss a demonstration. It does not purchase a service or subscribe you to a newsletter.
