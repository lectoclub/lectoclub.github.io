How to get started

  1. Create the club

    Download the app, choose “Bookshop or organisation” and add your shop’s link. Or write to us and we’ll help.

  2. Pick the book of the month

    Set the dates and the meeting, and open the discussion topics you want. Members get notified.

  3. Share the code

    At the counter, in your newsletter or on social media. Readers join with the code, for free.

Questions from bookshops

Do readers have to pay?

No. If the bookshop has the Club plan, every club member gets full access for free.

Will members lend each other books?

In bookshop clubs, lending between members is switched off. You can turn it on in the club options if you want.

Where do they buy the book?

From your bookshop. The club has a button that takes them to your website, and you can mention in the book’s note that it’s in stock.

We already have a WhatsApp group. What do we gain?

The book, dates, meeting and discussion for each book stay organised, with no spoilers mixed in and a history of everything you’ve read.

What reader data does the bookshop see?

The name members use in the club and what they post. You don’t see their email address or any payment details.
