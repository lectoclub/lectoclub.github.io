LectoClub privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Controller and contact

ArnoHealth S.L. (onmedic), Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes, 699, 2n 2a, 08013 Barcelona, Spain, is the controller of LectoClub data. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, write to info@onmedic.com with the subject “LectoClub privacy”.

2. Data we process

  • Adult account: email address, account ID, profile name and, if you choose one, a profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we may use the name and photo it provides. Google and Apple credentials are handled by those providers.
  • Libraries and clubs: the name of each library, its members, clubs, roles, invitation codes and the audience you choose when you start (school, readers or bookshop).
  • Children’s profiles (optional): first name or nickname and, if the adult adds it, year of birth. Children have no account or password.
  • Books and loans: ISBNs and editions, copies, ownership, condition, notes, cover photos you upload, the clubs you share them with, and the requests, dates and status of loans and reservations.
  • Reading and participation: books read, reading sessions and diary, ratings, comments in book discussions, discussion topics, polls, meetings and club activities, and the club book chosen by the organisers.
  • Subscriptions: if you buy a plan, the product, status, dates and transaction identifiers that Apple or Google send us to verify it. We never see or store payment details: the stores handle billing.
  • Operation: in-app notifications, your chosen language and, when the app hits an unexpected error, a technical report (error message, stack trace, platform, app version and language) linked to your account. Servers may process your IP address and request metadata to run and protect the service.

3. Purposes and legal basis

We process account, library, club and subscription data to provide the service you ask for (performance of a contract). The optional data in children’s profiles is provided by an adult with parental authority or sufficient authorisation, to organise the child’s reading; they can ask for it to be removed at any time. Error reports and technical data are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working. We keep data where the law requires it, for example data relating to purchases.

4. Who can see your data

  • Club members see the copies shared with the club, the name of the library they come from, and what is posted in the club: comments, ratings, polls, activities and the club book.
  • The books you have read are visible to whoever you choose: only you, your library or your clubs.
  • Nobody can search for or list clubs: you can only join with an invitation code.
  • We don’t sell personal data, there are no ads, and the app contains no behavioural analytics or advertising tools.

5. Providers

  • Supabase hosts authentication, the database and files in the Frankfurt region (EU).
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in when you choose them, and subscription purchases.
  • Open Library and Google Books receive the ISBN or search text to identify a book. Google Books requests go through our server, and covers may load from those sources’ servers.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under the applicable safeguards. Their policies: Supabase, Google, Apple and Internet Archive / Open Library.

6. Camera, photos and device data

The camera is optional: it reads ISBN and bookplate codes, and takes a cover or profile photo when you decide to. Codes are read on the device and no images are uploaded for that. Only photos you explicitly choose are uploaded. The device stores your session, a copy of the last library you viewed and cached covers; uninstalling the app doesn’t delete your account.

7. Children

The app is used by adults. We don’t ask for children’s addresses, phone numbers, location or surnames, and children have no profile photo. Use first names or nicknames and don’t put identifying details in notes, library names or comments. Don’t create profiles for minors without the appropriate authorisation.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep data while your account is active. Error reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can ask for your account to be deleted in the app, under “Account and privacy”, or by following the account deletion page. We process the request within 30 days. Subscriptions are cancelled from the store: deleting your account doesn’t stop them.

9. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw consent, by writing to info@onmedic.com. We reply within the legal deadline, usually one month. If you disagree with our answer, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

10. This website and changes

This website uses no tracking cookies. We’ll publish changes to this policy here and announce important ones in the app.
