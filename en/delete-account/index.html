Delete your LectoClub account

Last updated: November 21, 2026

You can ask for your LectoClub account and data to be deleted even if you no longer have the app installed.

In the app

  1. Sign in and open “Account and privacy”.
  2. Tap “Delete my account” and confirm the request.

We process it within 30 days and send you a confirmation. You can cancel it while it’s pending.

Without the app, or to delete specific data

  1. Email info@onmedic.com from your account’s email address with the subject “Delete LectoClub account”.
  2. Tell us whether you want to delete the whole account, a child’s profile or specific data. Don’t send passwords or documents.
  3. We’ll check the account is yours and confirm the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account and profile, your photo, your membership of libraries and clubs, and the related data. If a library is yours alone, its copies, children’s profiles and reading records are deleted too. If other people share the library, we keep their data and remove yours. You can ask us to delete just a child’s profile without closing your account.

Subscriptions and loans

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it first from your App Store or Google Play account. If you have open loans, we’ll contact you to sort them out, but that won’t stop the deletion.

What may be kept

We only keep data when the law requires it (for example, purchase records), and we’ll tell you what. General book records don’t identify anyone and are kept. Backups are purged on the provider’s schedule and are never used to restore deleted accounts.
