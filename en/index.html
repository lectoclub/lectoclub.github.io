One club, three ways to read

Schools and classes

A classroom library built from the books families already have at home.

  • Every copy has its own record and a QR bookplate, so it always finds its way back to its owner.
  • Loans with due dates, renewals and a waiting list.
  • Reading diary, goals without leaderboards and an end-of-year reading certificate.
  • Children have no account: they are profiles of the responsible adult.

Reading groups

For groups of friends and reading clubs of any age.

  • Share the books you have at home and lend them to each other.
  • Vote for the next book and schedule your meetings.
  • Read-alongs by chapter: each section’s discussion only opens once you get there.

Bookshops and organisations

Your bookshop’s reading club, with the book of the month and the discussion in every reader’s pocket.

  • The bookshop picks the club book, the dates and the meeting, and members get notified.
  • Discussion topics on the characters, the ending or the style, with spoiler warnings.
  • A history of past books, still open for comments.
  • A button to buy the book from the bookshop. No lending between members.
Everything your bookshop can do

How to get started

  1. Add your books

    Scan the ISBN with your camera, in bursts if you have plenty, or add a book by hand with a photo of the cover.

  2. Join the club

    The organiser creates it and shares a code. Each member chooses which books to share.

  3. Read and talk

    Request books, keep track of loans, vote for the next one and discuss it without spoilers.

Frequently asked questions

Does LectoClub sell or distribute books?

No. It organises physical books you already own. Loans and returns are arranged between members, and in bookshop clubs the book is bought at the bookshop.

Does everyone have to pay?

No. If the club has the Club plan, every member gets full access for free. The free plan already covers a small club.

What if two people own the same book?

Each copy is separate: the club sees two and knows which one is free.

Does it work offline?

You need internet to sign in and sync, but the app remembers the last library you viewed in case you lose signal.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, under “Account and privacy”, or by following the steps on the account deletion page.
