LectoClub help

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Getting started

  1. Sign in with email, Google or Apple, and choose how you’ll use LectoClub: with a school, a reading group or as a bookshop.
  2. Add your books by scanning the ISBN or entering them by hand.
  3. Create a club or join one with the invitation code you’ve been given, and choose which books to share.
  4. In the club you can request books, keep track of loans, vote for the next book and discuss it.

Bookshop clubs

The club’s organisers choose the club book, the dates and the meeting, and can open discussion topics. Members talk about the book and can buy it at the bookshop through the club’s link. Lending between members starts switched off.

Managing the library with another adult

In “Settings › Manage libraries”, tap the people icon on the library and “Invite another adult”. The other adult taps the key on the same screen and types the code. The code works once and expires after 7 days.

Plans

Plans are bought in the app, under “Settings”. To cancel or change a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account. If you’ve switched phones, use “Restore purchases”.

Contact

Write to lectoclub@onmedic.org with your app version, your phone and what happened. Don’t send passwords or children’s details; if you attach a screenshot, hide any personal data.
